# Expense reimbursement

Category: People

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Submit a claim

Submit expense claims in Finance Portal within 30 calendar days of the purchase. Attach an itemized receipt, business purpose, and project cost center. Claims without receipts require a written exception from Finance.

## Approval and payment

Your manager approves the business purpose and Finance checks the receipt and expense category. Approved reimbursements are paid in the next monthly payroll cycle. Finance returns incomplete claims with a request for missing information.

## Travel bookings

Get manager approval before booking business travel. Use the company travel portal for rail, flights, and hotels. Personal upgrades are not reimbursed unless approved in writing before booking.
