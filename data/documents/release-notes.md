# Writing release notes

Category: Product

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Audience and structure

Write release notes for customers using plain language. Explain what changed, who benefits, and whether the customer needs to take action. Group entries into New, Improved, and Fixed sections.

## Review

The product owner reviews release notes before publication. Engineering checks technical accuracy and Support checks that the wording is understandable. Avoid exposing internal identifiers, security-sensitive implementation details, or customer information.

## Publish

Publish approved release notes after the release owner confirms the deployment is healthy. Link the note from the product update feed and notify account owners when a requested improvement is included.
