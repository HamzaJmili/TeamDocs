# Meeting room handbook

Category: IT Support

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Reserve a room

Reserve a meeting room by adding its resource calendar to the event invitation. Include the meeting duration and expected number of attendees. The room is confirmed only when the resource calendar accepts the invitation.

## Video equipment

For video meetings, connect the room display, select the room microphone, and make a test call before guests arrive. Report broken cameras or microphones to IT Support with the room name.

## Room etiquette

Cancel unused room reservations as soon as plans change. Leave the room tidy and clear whiteboards containing sensitive material. Meetings should finish five minutes early to allow the next group to enter.
