# Northstar product overview

Category: Product

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## What we build

Northstar is a fictional workspace for small teams managing customer projects. Its core objects are workspaces, projects, tasks, and client updates. This product description exists only to make the TeamDocs demonstration realistic.

## Project lifecycle

A project moves through Draft, Active, In review, and Completed states. Project owners can archive completed projects to keep their workspace tidy. Archived projects remain searchable to members with access.

## Roles

Workspace administrators manage membership and settings. Project editors can update tasks and client updates. Viewers can read project content but cannot modify it. Product permissions are separate from engineering environment access.
