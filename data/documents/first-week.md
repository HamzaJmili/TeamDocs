# Your first week

Category: People

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Day one

During your first week, start by activating your company account, setting up multi-factor authentication, and reading the onboarding checklist. On day one, meet your onboarding buddy and confirm that your laptop, email, and calendar work. Your buddy is your first contact for everyday questions.

## Days two to five

By Friday of your first week, complete security awareness training, attend the product introduction, and agree on a small first task with your manager. Book a 30-minute check-in with your buddy. New engineers can request staging access after account setup.

## Finding help

Use the People Help channel for onboarding and workplace questions. Send equipment problems to IT Support. Your manager owns the first-month goals and should review them with you at the end of week one.
