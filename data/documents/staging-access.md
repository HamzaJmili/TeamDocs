# Staging access

Category: Engineering

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Request access

To request access to the staging environment, open an Access Desk ticket and choose the Staging role. Include your team, project, and the date you need access. Your engineering manager must approve the request before Platform Engineering grants it. Access is normally provisioned within two business days after approval.

## Connect safely

Before connecting to staging, enable multi-factor authentication and connect to the company VPN. Use your individual company account; shared logins are not allowed. Staging contains synthetic customer data only. If the VPN works but access is denied, add the error message to your Access Desk ticket.

## Access expiry

Staging access expires after 30 days. Request an extension in the original ticket with a new end date and manager approval. Staging access does not grant production access.
