# Time off and leave

Category: People

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Request annual leave

Request annual leave in People Portal at least ten working days before the proposed start date. Include the dates and a short handover note. Your manager approves the request after checking team coverage. Do not treat a submitted request as approved leave.

## Unexpected absence

For unexpected absence, notify your manager as soon as practical and record the absence in People Portal. Sensitive medical details should not be posted in team channels. People Operations can explain any documentation required.

## Handover

Before approved leave, nominate a backup for active work, update project notes, and add your absence to the shared team calendar. Engineers with on-call duties must arrange a confirmed rota swap.
