# Customer feedback workflow

Category: Product

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Capture feedback

Record customer feedback in the Feedback Board with a short summary, source channel, and affected product area. Remove personal identifiers from shared notes. Link related feedback to avoid creating duplicate feature requests.

## Prioritization

The product team reviews feedback every Wednesday. Priority considers customer impact, frequency, strategic fit, and implementation effort. A feature request is not a delivery commitment until it appears in an approved release plan.

## Close the loop

When a requested improvement ships, the account owner informs the customer and links the release note. If a request is declined, explain the reason respectfully and keep the feedback record for future review.
