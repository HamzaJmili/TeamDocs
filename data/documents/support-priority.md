# Customer support priorities

Category: Product

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Priority definitions

P1 means a customer cannot use a core workflow and no workaround exists. P2 means a significant issue has a workaround. P3 covers minor issues and general questions. Security concerns are escalated to Security Desk regardless of support priority.

## Response targets

During support hours, the first-response target is one hour for P1, four hours for P2, and two business days for P3. These are internal planning targets in a fictional demo, not contractual service guarantees.

## Escalation

Escalate unresolved P1 cases to the support lead and engineering on-call. Include the affected workflow, reproduction steps, timestamps, and sanitized error details. Keep the customer updated when status changes.
