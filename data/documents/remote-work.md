# Remote work guide

Category: People

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Working hours

Remote team members should be reachable during core collaboration hours from 10:00 to 15:00 UTC, excluding their scheduled break. Discuss timezone constraints with your manager. The remaining working hours are flexible within your local contract.

## Work location

Before working from another country, obtain written approval from People Operations. Give at least four weeks notice and provide the location and dates. Approval depends on employment, tax, and information-security considerations.

## Communication

Keep your calendar and status up to date. Prefer written project updates for non-urgent decisions. If a discussion changes a decision, record the outcome in the project document so colleagues in other timezones can follow it.
