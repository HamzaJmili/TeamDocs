# Account recovery

Category: IT Support

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Reset your password

To reset your account password, use the identity portal self-service reset flow and verify with your registered second factor. Choose a unique password and store it in the approved password manager. IT Support will never ask you to share your password.

## Lost second factor

If your authentication device is lost, contact IT Support using the recovery contact process. Identity verification is required before registering a new second factor. Do not ask a colleague to share their account.

## Locked account

An account is temporarily locked after five failed sign-in attempts. Wait 15 minutes before trying again, or contact IT Support if the problem persists. Report unexpected sign-in notifications to Security Desk.
