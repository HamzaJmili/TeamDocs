# VPN troubleshooting

Category: IT Support

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Connection checklist

If the VPN will not connect, first check your internet connection, restart the VPN client, and verify that your device clock is correct. Confirm your multi-factor authentication request has not expired. Do not disable endpoint protection to make the connection work.

## Repeated failures

After two failed VPN connection attempts, open an IT Support ticket. Include the operating system, VPN client version, timestamp, and exact error code. Never include your password or recovery codes.

## Connected but denied

A successful VPN connection does not grant application permissions. If staging access is denied while the VPN is connected, check whether your staging role has expired and update your Access Desk ticket.
