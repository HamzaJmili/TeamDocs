# Learning and development

Category: People

Fictional Northstar demonstration content. Not a real company policy.

## Learning allowance

The fictional Northstar learning allowance is 600 euros per employee per calendar year. It covers approved books, courses, and professional events related to your role. Unused allowance does not carry over into the next year.

## Course approval

Before buying a course, send your manager the course link, cost, and learning objective. After approval, submit the receipt through the expense process and choose Learning as the category. Keep a short note on how the course relates to your work.

## Share your learning

After completing a course or conference, share three useful takeaways with your team. A short written note or a ten-minute presentation is enough. Paid study leave is not specified by this guide.
